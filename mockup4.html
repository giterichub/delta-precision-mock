The Reality

Your production backlog is not a staffing problem. It's a capacity problem.

Technical teams get stretched. Deadlines stay fixed. Senior engineers and technical staff can end up spending time on production work that a dedicated technical team could handle — because there's nobody else to do it.

Hiring solves it eventually. But hiring takes months, costs more than the work justifies, and leaves you with fixed headcount when the project is done. Most firms either carry the cost or keep the backlog.

Delta is the third option. A managed production team that learns your standards, handles the defined workflow, and takes the day-to-day production management off your team. You stay connected on priorities, requirements, and feedback. We manage the people, production, QA, and performance. Delta removes the burden of building and operating the production workforce, so you can focus your time and resources on what moves your business forward.

What Sets Delta Apart

Not a vendor. A managed production operation.

Delta doesn't just provide people. We manage the production system around the work — the training, the QA, the documentation, the performance tracking, and the scaling. Here is what that looks like in practice.

Training infrastructure

Every team member trains against documented standards before touching live work. Not a one-day briefing — a structured ramp-up with supervised production.

Dedicated QA

QA is built into the operation, not bolted on. Reviewers check work by asset type before anything is submitted. Internal QA is completed before delivery, with failed work returned for correction.

Knowledge management

Edge cases, requirement changes and workflow decisions are documented. When new people join, they train against a written standard — not one person's memory.

Performance tracking

An internal dashboard tracks completion rates, QA pass rates, turnaround, and remediation at the individual level. Targeted benchmarks, backed by data.

Client feedback loop

Your feedback changes the workflow. Requirement updates are documented, retrained, and tracked — not acknowledged and quietly ignored.

Scalability

The team expands when your workload grows and contracts when it doesn't. No hiring cycle, no severance, no idle bench time on your budget.
