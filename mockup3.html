02 / The Capacity Gap

Your production backlog is not a staffing problem. It's a capacity problem.

Technical teams get stretched. Deadlines stay fixed. Senior engineers and technical staff can end up spending time on production work that a dedicated technical team could handle — because there's nobody else to do it.

Hiring solves it eventually. But hiring takes months, costs more than the work justifies, and leaves you with fixed headcount when the project is done. Most firms either carry the cost or keep the backlog.

Delta is the third option. A managed production team that learns your standards, handles the defined workflow, and takes the day-to-day production management off your team. You stay connected on priorities, requirements, and feedback. We manage the people, production, QA, and performance.

Delta removes the burden of building and operating the production workforce, so you can focus your time and resources on what moves your business forward.

06 / The Operating Model

How it works.

Our core values of integrity, excellence, and tenacity are built into everything we do for our partners, right down to the operating model Delta uses to build and manage your technical production workflows.

01

Understand

We learn your workflow, standards, quality benchmarks, and what a good deliverable looks like before a single file is opened.

02

Build the team

We assemble the right people for the specific work, matched to the technical domain, not pulled from a generic pool.

03

Train and calibrate

Every team member trains against documented standards and works supervised production before handling live files.

04

Produce

Work is distributed, tracked against productivity benchmarks, and managed internally, not handed back to you to supervise.

05

Internal QA

Dedicated QA reviewers check every file before it leaves Delta. Internal QA is completed before delivery.

06

Client review and feedback

Feedback from your team is fed back into workflow documentation and training, not absorbed and forgotten.

07

Measure and improve

Performance, accuracy, and turnaround are tracked at the individual level.

08

Scale

When your workload grows, the team grows with it. When it contracts, so does the team.
